> If I'm sitting in front of my camera talking about, yeah, three months ago, I wanted to do a lot with the boys.. that's one thing. What if you still case that? We just can draw your audience alongside the journey.. Do you see how much more engaged they are?

Document your authentic daily lifestyle (morning routine, gym, gratitude) as content because it becomes effortless to produce and naturally attracts aligned clients.

> When he becomes part of you, how effortless is it now? Feel to document your journey, bro. Super easy, bro. It's like, it's me, bro.

Document your visible progress over time—show your old content and results from years ago alongside current results to allow the audience to see your transformation and believe in their own potential.

> the best thing that you can ever do, the best business model is capture your progress
