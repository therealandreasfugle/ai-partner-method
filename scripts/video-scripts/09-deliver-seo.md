## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> Being found by people who do not know their name yet. The good news is you build
> almost none of this. It comes out of the site build automatically.

---

## SCREEN: what is already automatic

Open a built site and show these in the page source or the built output.

TALK ABOUT:
- Structured data so Google understands what the business is: organisation, local
  business, services, FAQs, locations and breadcrumbs.
- A page for every service area they cover. That is the single largest part of
  ranking locally.
- A page for every service.
- Sitemap and robots file.
- Speed, gated so the site passes the Core Web Vitals check.

---

## SCREEN: what AEO honestly means

TALK ABOUT:
- More people are asking an AI assistant for a local plumber instead of scrolling
  Google.
- Whether a business gets named depends on the site being readable and quotable.
- The FAQ structured data is what does that, and it is readable without
  JavaScript. The pretty accordion on the page is not.

---

## YOU MUST CUSTOMISE

- Every service area they actually cover, from their form
- Their real services
- The FAQ answers, taken from what their customers genuinely ask them

**SAY THIS:**

> Do not pad the service areas with towns they do not cover. Ranking for work they
> cannot take is worse than not ranking, because now they are turning people down
> and looking unreliable doing it.

---

## KNOWN GAP, tell them straight

**SAY THIS:**

> One honest gap. Right now the individual page titles and descriptions inherit
> the homepage. Set the homepage ones properly, and if a client cares about one
> specific service page, set that one by hand.

---

## THE ONE THING

**SAY THIS:**

> Never promise a client that an AI will recommend them. You cannot control that
> and you cannot check it. What you can say is that you are structuring their site
> so it is readable and quotable. That is the honest claim, and it is enough to
> sell the line item.
