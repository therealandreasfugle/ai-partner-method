# Video 10: Deliver line 05, the referral program

**Length:** 4 minutes. **Value on the proposal:** $900.
**Source:** `04-deliver/05-referral`. **System:** `tools/crm/referrals.html`,
`tools/crm/api/referrals.js`, `tools/crm/thanks.html`.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> Their existing customers sending them the next ones. This one is already built
> further than the proposal line promises, so you are mostly setting numbers.

---

## SCREEN: what is already built

Open the referrals page in the CRM and point at each thing.

TALK ABOUT:
- A payout ledger, with pending and paid.
- Cash or credit, chosen per referral.
- A yearly cash cap per person, enforced on the server, so a referrer never
  crosses the threshold that creates a tax reporting headache for your client.
- Self referral blocking and a duplicate guard.
- Three automated emails: welcome, pending and paid.
- Reward, discount, keyword and cap are all editable in the CRM with no
  redeploying.

---

## YOU MUST CUSTOMISE, per client

- The reward amount, and cash or credit
- The discount the referred customer gets
- The keyword customers quote

---

## THE ONE THING

**SAY THIS:**

> The client funds the payout, not you. It is in the contract, but say it out loud
> on the sales call anyway, because the first time somebody gets referred and
> there is a hundred pounds to pay out, you do not want that to be a surprise
> conversation.
>
> And set the numbers so they work for that client's margin. The line that sells
> it is: nobody turns down paying out a hundred to bring in a thousand. If their
> numbers do not support that, do not run the programme for them at all.
