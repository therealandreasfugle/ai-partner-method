# Video 1: What you actually get

**Length:** 8 minutes. **Source:** `WHAT-YOU-GET.md`.
**Goal:** they see the finished thing working before they set up a single account,
so they know what they are working towards.

Record this one first and publish it first. It is the video that stops people
refunding on day one because they opened a folder of code and panicked.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> Before you set up anything, I want you to see what you are actually selling.
> Everything I am about to open is live right now. You are not building any of
> it. Your job is to connect it to your own accounts and then run the process.

---

## SCREEN: six tabs, open them one at a time

Have all six loaded before you record so nothing spins.

**Tab 1:** https://aipm-templates.vercel.app

Scroll the gallery. Open two of them.

TALK ABOUT:
- Twenty finished websites, all yours.
- One shared core underneath, twenty different skins on top.
- You pick the closest one to the client's trade, fill in their real details, and
  deploy it. You are not designing anything from scratch.

**Tab 2:** https://summit-roofing-site.vercel.app

This is the one that sells the course. Do these three things on camera:

1. Scroll the whole page slowly.
2. Submit the enquiry form. Actually submit it.
3. Click the chat bubble and ask it something specific, like whether they cover a
   town it serves.

TALK ABOUT while the chat answers:
- The chatbot already knows the services, the area, the hours and the phone
  number, because that got written in at build time.
- The form does not just show a thank you page. It emails the owner, posts to
  their Slack, and sends the customer an instant reply.

**Tab 3:** https://aipm-client-proposal.vercel.app

Scroll the whole thing without commentary for about fifteen seconds, then talk.

TALK ABOUT:
- This is what you put on screen during a sales call.
- The prices are set by you in a panel on the page.
- They sign the contract on the page and pay, while you are still on the call.

**Tab 4:** https://aipm-instant-site.vercel.app

TALK ABOUT:
- Built from a Google Maps listing in about forty five seconds, before you have
  spoken to the business at all.
- This is your cold outreach. Not a pitch, a finished site with their name on it.

**Tab 5:** https://aipm-instant-proposal.vercel.app/proposal.html?site=integrity-plumbing-heating

TALK ABOUT:
- Same idea for the proposal. One scraped lead in, one proposal out, with their
  own site embedded in it.
- This is the link that goes in the cold email.

**Tab 6:** https://settoku-watch.vercel.app

TALK ABOUT:
- A video page you send instead of doing a live call for every single lead.
- Useful once you have more leads than hours.

## THE ONE THING

**SAY THIS:**

> Everything I just showed you is running on our accounts, as a demonstration.
> You deploy your own copies, on your own hosting, with your own keys. Nothing
> you ever build touches anyone else's client data, and nothing you build
> depends on us staying online.

---

## SCREEN: the repo, top level only

Open the folder. Show only the top level list. Do not open a code file.

TALK ABOUT, pointing at each folder as you say it:
- `00-setup`, you do once.
- `01` through `05`, you live in on repeat.
- `tools` is the code. You will almost never open it.
- `scripts` is every word you will ever need to send or say.
- `.claude` is the commands. You type these and the AI does the work.

**SAY THIS:**

> Five commands run this entire business. Slash setup, slash check, slash find
> leads, slash build site, slash onboard client. If you never learn anything else
> in this repo, learn those five.
