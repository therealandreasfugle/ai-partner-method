# 01. The website build

**What the client is paying for:** a real website that gets found and turns
visitors into booked jobs. Value on the proposal: $4,500.

**Where the system lives:** `tools/website-templates` (20 finished templates) and
`tools/website-template` (the one the factory builds on).

## What you do

1. Read their onboarding form answers.
2. Pick the closest of the 20 templates. Match the trade first, then the feel they
   asked for. If nothing matches their trade, see
   [REUSING-A-TEMPLATE.md](REUSING-A-TEMPLATE.md), a template is a skin and any
   of them can become any trade in about twenty minutes.
3. Fill in their real details: services, service areas, hours, phone, reviews,
   photos, logo.
4. Deploy to a new Vercel project of their own.
5. Point their domain at it.

Or just run `/build-site` in Claude Code and it does the above from their form.

## You MUST customise

- Every service and service area, from their form
- Their phone, email, address and hours
- Their real photos. Stock photos of other people's work is the fastest way to
  look like every other cheap site
- Their real reviews

## Check before you hand it over

- Open it at desktop and phone width
- Submit the enquiry form yourself and confirm the alert email arrives
- Every link goes somewhere real
- No template text left anywhere

⚠️ **Never invent a review, a rating, a year founded, a customer count or an
accreditation.** If the form left it blank, ask or leave it off.
