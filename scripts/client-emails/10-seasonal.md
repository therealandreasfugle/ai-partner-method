# 10. Seasonal check in

**Send:** once or twice a year, in the week before their busy season starts.
**Goal:** be the supplier who turns up before they need you.

This is the highest value email in this folder per minute spent. Put the dates in
your calendar the day you sign each client.

---

**Subject:** Getting you ready for [season]

Hi [First name],

[Storm season / the first frost / MOT season] usually kicks off around now, so I
went through the site and got a few things ready:

- Put [the emergency callout / the seasonal service] front and centre on the
  homepage
- Added a page for [the seasonal job people search for]
- Checked the enquiry form and the phone number are working

Nothing needed from you. Just wanted you set up before the phone starts going.

If there's a particular job you want more of this year, tell me and I'll push it.

[Your name]

---

## When to send it, by trade

| Trade | Send before |
|---|---|
| Roofers | Storm season, and the first proper autumn wind |
| Plumbers and heating | The first cold snap, and again before Christmas |
| Garages | MOT season, and before the summer holidays |
| Landscapers and gardeners | Early spring |
| Electricians | Autumn, when the lights go on and the fuseboards trip |
| Drives, fencing, external work | Early spring, and after the first bad storm |

## Notes

- Do the work before you send the email. This is not asking their permission, it
  is telling them you already did it. That is what makes it land.
- One email in the right week of the year beats twelve generic ones, because it
  proves you are thinking about their business when they are not paying you any
  attention.
- The last line is a soft opening for extra work without pitching anything.
