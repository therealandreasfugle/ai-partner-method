# 5. Keep the client

The build fee pays once. The monthly fee is the business. Most people lose it not
because the work was bad, but because the client stopped hearing from them and
started wondering what they were paying for.

## The only rule that matters

**Send something every month, whether or not they ask.** A client who hears from
you monthly stays for years. A client who hears nothing cancels the first quiet
month they have.

## What to send

Four numbers, in plain English, not a dashboard:

1. How many people found the site, and whether that went up
2. How many enquired, which is the only number that becomes money
3. Where they came from
4. What you changed this month, and what you are doing next month

Everything you need is in `04-deliver/10-analytics`.

## The edits

Priority edits are part of what they pay for. Beyond the included hour, it is
charged hourly, and that is on the proposal.

⚠️ Answer edit requests fast even when they are small. A same-day reply to
"can you change the phone number" buys more loyalty than any report.

## Seasonal work

The most valuable thing you can do is turn up before they need you. A roofer
before storm season, a plumber before the first freeze, a garage before the MOT
rush. One email in the right week each year is worth more than twelve generic
ones.
