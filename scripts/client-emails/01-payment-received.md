# 01. Payment received

**Send:** within the hour their payment clears. Not tomorrow.
**Goal:** they fill in the onboarding form today, while they are still excited.

---

**Subject:** You're all set, [Business name]

Hi [First name],

Payment's through, thanks. Here's what happens next.

I need about ten minutes of your time to get everything I need for the build.
It's one form, and it's the only one you'll ever have to fill in for me:

[onboarding form link]

The bit that takes longest is the photos, so if you can point me at a folder,
a Google Drive, or even just your camera roll, that covers it. Photos of your
actual work beat anything else you could give me.

Once that's back, I'll start building. I'll send you a link to look at before
anything goes live.

Anything you're unsure about on the form, just leave it and I'll ask.

[Your name]

---

## Notes

- Send this in the same message that confirms the sale, not as a separate email
  an hour later.
- The photos line is there because photos are the single most common reason a
  build stalls. Ask for them in the first email, every time.
- Do not attach the contract again. They signed it, they have it.
