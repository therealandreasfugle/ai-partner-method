Implement a three-step sales team structure: (1) DM setter/closer to collect $15k-$30k upfront from warm DMs, (2) dialer to qualify and book calls from funnel leads, (3) closer to close calls; have a sales manager oversee all three, running daily calls to improve objection handling and close rates.

> I have a cop writer who writes like the BSL's, the emails, etc. I have a script writer who writes the content. I have a V.A. who does all the back-end work. I have a marketer and a CSM who is my business partner. I have a project manager which is coming or I don't have one yet.

> when it gets really fun because it's virtually just going to be no closing calls no sales through it can be just fulfillment and posting yes it's like we're both an extent

> Now you have three components you have your offer. You have your acquisition and you have your fulfillment. Now this operating system is what is called a chain mechanism and that chain is technically a system and that system is technically your business.

Only pay revenue share (e.g., 10% over incremental revenue) to team members who drive exponential business growth through high-leverage activities; don't pay share on baseline revenue or routine external tasks.

Prioritize hiring for the roles you cannot execute at a high level first (VA, copywriter), then hire for roles you can already execute well (sales manager)—this accelerates growth faster than the reverse order.

> I would have hired a VA I would have hired a copyrighted then I would have hired a sales manager because I could do sales management to a high to a high level to a high degree but I couldn't do those other two things to a high degree
