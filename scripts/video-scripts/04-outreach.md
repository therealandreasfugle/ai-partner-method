# Video 4: Outreach that books calls

**Length:** 15 minutes. **Source:** `02-outreach`, `scripts/cold-email`,
`scripts/dm`, `scripts/phone/cold-call.md`, `02-outreach/email-warmup.md`.
**Goal:** they send their first real outreach the same day they watch this.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> Every single thing in this video has one job. Get them on a call. Not a reply,
> not a conversation, not a quote. A call in your calendar. If a message does not
> end in book a call, it is not outreach, it is a chat.

---

## SCREEN: the three shapes, from `03-sell/README.md`

TALK ABOUT:
- A proposal with their own site embedded in it, then book a call.
- A video plus that proposal, then book a call.
- A video on its own, then book a call.

Pick one per lead. All three end the same way.

---

## THE ONE THING, part one

**SAY THIS:**

> We never ask a prospect to fill in a form. Ever. A busy owner is not answering
> questions for a stranger they have not decided to trust yet. The only form
> anybody ever fills in is the onboarding form, and that goes out after they have
> paid you.

---

## SCREEN: build the outreach asset

Open the instant builder and run it against one of the leads from video 3.

TALK ABOUT while it builds:
- About forty five seconds, from a Google Maps listing to a real site with their
  name on it.
- This is the whole hook. You are not saying you could build them a site. You
  already did.
- The proposal comes out of the same run, with their existing site embedded in it
  so the comparison makes itself.

---

## SCREEN: the cold emails

Open `scripts/cold-email` and show the folder listing.

TALK ABOUT:
- Four angles. No website, outdated site, reviews, and not ranking.
- Pick the angle that matches what the scraper said about that specific business.
  The angle is already in the lead row.
- Follow ups and a break up email are in there too. Most replies come from the
  follow ups, not the first send.

Open one angle file and read the first two lines out loud, then stop.

**SAY THIS:**

> Short. Their name, one specific thing about their business, the link, and the
> ask. Nobody reads paragraph two of an email from a stranger.

---

## SCREEN: DMs and the phone

Open `scripts/dm/README.md` briefly.

Open `scripts/phone/cold-call.md`.

TALK ABOUT:
- The cold call script and the voicemail.
- The dialler in your CRM runs through your own phone, so there is nothing to set
  up and nothing billed to you.

---

## THE ONE THING, part two

This is the part that decides whether any of it works. Slow down.

**SAY THIS:**

> The thing that kills most people here is not the copy. It is deliverability.
> A brand new domain sending fifty cold emails on day one lands in spam
> permanently, and it looks exactly like a copy problem, because the test emails
> you send yourself arrive perfectly.
>
> Two rules. Ramp up. Five a day in week one, build to fifty over a month. And
> use two domains: never send cold email from the same domain your clients reply
> to. If you burn it, you burn the address your paying clients use.

Show `02-outreach/email-warmup.md` on screen while you say it, and point at the
mail tester check at the end.

---

## CLOSE

**SAY THIS:**

> Send twenty tomorrow, not two hundred. Next video is the call itself, which is
> the only part of this business that is genuinely a skill.
