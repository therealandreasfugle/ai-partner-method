# Video 17: Your own credibility

**Length:** 8 minutes. **Source:** `06-your-own-credibility`.
**Goal:** they build their own site in an afternoon instead of avoiding it for
six months.

This is the most commonly skipped part of the whole business. Say that out loud.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> You are selling websites. Not having one is the loudest thing about you. Every
> owner you contact is going to look you up before they get on a call, and what
> they find decides whether they turn up.

---

## SCREEN: the three things, in order

**One. Your own site.**

TALK ABOUT:
- Use one of the twenty templates. You already own them.
- Give yourself an afternoon, not a week.
- It needs four things: what you do, who for, a few examples, and a way to book a
  call. That is the whole list.

**SAY THIS:**

> A perfect site you never launch is worth exactly nothing. Launch the good enough
> one this week and improve it with money coming in.

**Two. Proof you can show.**

TALK ABOUT:
- You are allowed to show the demo builds in this repo as examples of the work you
  deliver.
- Once you have real clients, swap them out for those.
- Screenshots of real sites you built beat any sentence you can write.

**Three. Somewhere they can check you exist.**

TALK ABOUT:
- A Google Business Profile of your own.
- One social profile you actually post on. One, posted on, beats four abandoned.
- A real email address on your own domain. Not a free webmail address.

---

## THE ONE THING

**SAY THIS:**

> Never invent a testimonial, a client count, or a case study result. You are
> about to spend a year telling local business owners that being honest beats
> marketing gloss. Doing the opposite on your own site is both wrong and the
> easiest thing in the world to get caught doing.
>
> "I build websites for local businesses" is true on your first day. "Trusted by
> fifty businesses" is not, and one ordinary follow up question on a call ends the
> deal and the reputation at the same time.

---

## SCREEN: the honest first pitch

**SAY THIS:**

> You do not need years of history to sell this. You need one thing they cannot
> argue with, and you already have it: you built them a website before you called
> them. Nobody else who contacts that business this year will have done that.
>
> That is the proof. Everything else is decoration.

---

## CLOSE

> That is the whole thing. Set up once, then find them, contact them, sell them,
> build it, keep them. Go and pick a town.
