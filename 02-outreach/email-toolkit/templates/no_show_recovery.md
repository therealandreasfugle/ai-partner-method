# No-Show Recovery (3 emails)

**Goal:** Reschedule the missed appointment without making them feel guilty.

**Trigger:** Lead was a no-show on a booked call.

**Recommended framework:** Direct, low-pressure. Lighter on the third email.

---

## Email 1 — "We Missed You"

**Send:** Within 1 hour of the missed call
**Goal:** Acknowledge without guilt-tripping. Make rescheduling frictionless.

```
Subject: We missed you at [Time]

Hey [First Name],

Looks like you couldn't make our [Time] call — life happens.

If you want to grab a new time, here's my calendar: [Link]

Quick reminder of why we were going to chat:
[1 sentence — their stated goal or pain from the original booking]

No worries either way.

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: "Life happens" removes the guilt. Single CTA — calendar link. The 1-line reminder of *why* they booked rekindles the original motivation.

---

## Email 2 — Value Add

**Send:** Day 2 (~36h after no-show)
**Framework:** Educational
**Goal:** Re-engage with content, soft re-pitch the call.

```
Subject: While you decide

Hey [First Name],

While you're thinking about whether to grab another call slot, I wanted to send something useful.

[Specific resource — short video, case study, article — that addresses the lead's stated pain point. Be specific in the framing: "Sarah's [audience type] was stuck at [their level]. Here's the system that got her to [result]."]

[Resource Link]

If anything in there clicks, you can grab a time here: [Calendar Link]

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: Day 2 is when they'd usually go silent. Giving genuine value re-opens the conversation without pressure. The case study should be from a client they can see themselves in.

---

## Email 3 — Final Attempt

**Send:** Day 5
**Goal:** Get a yes or a clean exit.

```
Subject: Last note about your call

Hey [First Name],

I'm going to close out your file unless I hear back.

If you want to talk: [Calendar Link] — pick anything that works.

If now isn't the right time: just hit reply with "not now" and I'll move on.

Hope you're well either way.

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: Honest, low-pressure binary. They either book or release the slot. No guilt trip, no manufactured urgency. Surprisingly effective because it's so different from the typical "ARE YOU STILL INTERESTED??!" email everyone else sends.

---

## Customization checklist

- [ ] Email 1 sends within 1 hour of the missed call (automate this — manual = forgotten).
- [ ] Calendar link works and shows availability for the next 7+ days.
- [ ] "Why we were going to chat" line is from their actual booking form / call notes — not generic.
- [ ] Email 2's resource is specific to their stated pain, not a generic "5 tips" article.
- [ ] Email 3 sends only if they didn't reschedule from #1 or #2 (ESP automation).

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                             | Fix                                                          |
|-----------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| "You missed our call!" subject line                 | Sounds accusatory. "We missed you" reframes blame off them.  |
| Sending Email 1 the next day                        | First hour is the highest-recovery window. Automate it.       |
| Email 2 that re-pitches the offer hard              | They no-showed once. Hard-pitching loses them for good. Stick to value. |
| 5+ recovery emails                                  | 3 is the line. After that you're spamming, not following up. |
| Continuing the sequence after they reschedule       | ESP must tag them as "rescheduled" and exclude from the rest. |
