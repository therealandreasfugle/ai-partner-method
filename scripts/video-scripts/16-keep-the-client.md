# Video 16: Keeping the client

**Length:** 8 minutes. **Source:** `05-keep-the-client`.
**Goal:** they understand that the monthly fee is the actual business and they
know exactly what to send.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> The build fee pays you once. The monthly fee is the business. And most people
> lose the monthly fee not because the work was bad, but because the client
> stopped hearing from them and started wondering what they were paying for.

TALK ABOUT:
- Do the maths out loud. Ten clients on a monthly fee is a real income before you
  sell anything new that month.
- Every client you lose is one you have to replace before you grow at all.

---

## THE ONE THING

Say this early, because it is the whole video.

**SAY THIS:**

> Send them something every single month, whether or not they ask for it. A client
> who hears from you monthly stays for years. A client who hears nothing cancels
> the first quiet month they have. That is it. That is the entire retention
> strategy and it beats anything clever.

---

## SCREEN: what to send

Four numbers, in plain English, not a dashboard.

1. How many people found the site, and whether that went up.
2. How many enquired, which is the only number that becomes money.
3. Where they came from.
4. What you changed this month, and what you are doing next month.

TALK ABOUT:
- Number four is the one that keeps you paid. The first three prove the site is
  working. Number four proves you are working.
- Even in a quiet month you have something for number four. Speed check, a new
  service page, updated seasonal copy.

---

## SCREEN: the edits

TALK ABOUT:
- Priority edits are part of what they pay for. Past the included hour it is
  charged hourly, and that is on the proposal so it is never a surprise.

**SAY THIS:**

> Answer the small edits fast, even the tiny ones. A same day reply to "can you
> change the phone number" buys you more loyalty than any report you will ever
> send. It takes you ninety seconds and it is the thing they tell other business
> owners about.

---

## SCREEN: seasonal work

**SAY THIS:**

> The most valuable thing you can do is turn up before they need you. A roofer
> before storm season. A plumber before the first freeze. A garage before the MOT
> rush. One email in the right week of the year is worth more than twelve generic
> ones, because it proves you are thinking about their business when they are not
> paying attention.

Suggest they put those dates in a calendar the day they sign the client.

---

## STILL MISSING, say it straight

**SAY THIS:**

> Two things are not written yet. The monthly report template and the win back
> sequence for a client who has drifted. Use the four numbers above until those
> land, and honestly, four numbers in an email beats a template anyway.

---

## CLOSE

> Last video, and it is the one people skip. Your own credibility.
