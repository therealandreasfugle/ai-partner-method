# 05. Launch day

**Send:** the day it goes live on their own address, after you have opened it
yourself and seen the padlock.
**Goal:** they understand everything they now own, and the monthly fee makes
sense from day one.

---

**Subject:** You're live

Hi [First name],

[their domain] is live.

Quick rundown of what you've now got running:

- **Your website**, on your own address, secure
- **Enquiries come straight to you.** Anyone who fills in the form emails you
  within seconds, and you can just hit reply to answer them
- **They get an instant reply too**, so nobody's sat wondering if you got it
- **The chat on the site** answers questions about your services and hours
  around the clock, and pushes people to get in touch
- **Review requests** go out automatically after a finished job
- **Your referral page** is at [referral link], send that to your regulars

One thing worth doing today: send yourself an enquiry through the form on your
phone, so you know what it looks like when a real one comes in.

I'll send you the first month's numbers on [date].

[Your name]

---

## Notes

- Do not send this until you have opened the live domain yourself and confirmed
  it is serving the new site with a padlock. A deploy message is not proof.
- Naming the date of the first report sets the monthly expectation immediately.
  Put it in your own calendar as you send this.
- The "send yourself an enquiry" line gets them to experience the fast follow-up
  themselves, which is the thing they are actually paying monthly for.
