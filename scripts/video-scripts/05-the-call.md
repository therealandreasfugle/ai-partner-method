# Video 5: The call, the proposal, the money

**Length:** 15 minutes. **Source:** `03-sell`, `scripts/phone/discovery-call.md`,
`tools/sales-proposal`.
**Goal:** they can run the call without freezing, and they know exactly where the
money gets taken.

This is the last video in the first module. After this one a student can
technically go and earn.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> This is the only part of the business that is a skill rather than a process.
> The good news is the proposal does most of the closing for you. Your job on the
> call is to ask six questions and then shut up.

---

## SCREEN: the proposal, https://aipm-client-proposal.vercel.app

Scroll the entire thing top to bottom in silence for about twenty seconds. Let
them see the length of it.

TALK ABOUT as you scroll back through:
- This is on screen while you talk. You share your screen, they follow along.
- The ten line items are all here with what each one is worth.
- The prices come from a seller panel you set. Show it.
- The contract is at the bottom and they sign it on the page.

---

## SCREEN: the seven steps

Open `scripts/phone/discovery-call.md` and put the seven steps on screen. Talk
through them without reading them out word for word.

1. Agree up front that a yes or a no today are both fine. This takes the pressure
   out of the room and it makes step five survivable.
2. Ask the six questions about how they get work now, and then be quiet. Longest
   silence you can stand.
3. Say their own answers back to them and get them to agree that is the situation.
4. Walk the growth equation, tying every lever to something they just told you.
   Not a feature list.
5. Say the price and stop talking. Do not fill the gap. Whoever speaks first
   loses.
6. Handle the real objection, not the wrapper. "I need to think about it" is
   almost never about thinking.
7. Take the payment and the signature while you are still on the call.

**SAY THIS:**

> Step five is the one everyone fails. You will say the number and then, because
> the silence is unbearable, you will start justifying it. Say the number. Then
> nothing.

---

## THE ONE THING

Both halves of this matter. The first keeps them out of trouble, the second keeps
them paid.

**SAY THIS:**

> Never promise a result. Not double the calls, not a number of leads, not a
> ranking. You are selling a mechanism and a standard of work, and you keep going
> until they are happy. That is what the agreement says and it is what keeps you
> out of a dispute you cannot win.
>
> And work starts after the money clears. Not when they say they will pay. The
> non refundable clause in that contract only protects you if you keep that order,
> and the first time you build before you are paid is the time they disappear.

---

## SCREEN: taking the money

Show the signature and payment part of the proposal.

TALK ABOUT:
- Set your payment links in Stripe. `00-setup/10-stripe.md`.
- The build fee is once. The monthly fee is the actual business.
- Do not discount the monthly to win the build. You will regret it every month
  for a year.

## SCREEN: the moment they pay

- It confirms the money actually cleared, sends the onboarding form the same day,
