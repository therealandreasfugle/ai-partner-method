# Angle A: they have no website

Use when the scrape found a phone number and a Google listing but no site. This
is the easiest angle you will ever work, because the gap is obvious to them too.
They already know they should have one. They have not because it sounded
expensive, slow, and like a hassle.

So do not sell them a website. Remove the reasons they have not got one.

---

## Email 1: the one that gets the reply

**Subject:** Built this for {{business}}

Hi,

I build websites for {{trade}} businesses around {{town}} and I put a version
together for {{business}} so you could see it rather than imagine it.

It is here: {{site}}

Nothing to sign up for, and I have not put it anywhere public. If it is close,
I can have it live on your own address in a few days. If it is not for you, no
hard feelings, just tell me and I will leave you alone.

{{your_name}}
{{your_phone}}

> **Why this works.** You did the work before asking for anything. Almost nobody
> does that, so it does not read like the other twenty emails in their inbox.
> Keep "no hard feelings" in. It lowers the cost of replying, and a no is worth
> more than silence because it clears your pipeline.

---

## Email 2: the practical one

**Subject:** Quick one about {{business}}

Hi,

When somebody in {{town}} searches for what you do, they get a list. The ones
with a website get looked at, and the ones without get scrolled past, even when
they are the better outfit.

I already built you a working one to look at: {{site}}

Worth five minutes?

{{your_name}}

> **Why this works.** It names the loss without insulting them. "Even when they
> are the better outfit" is the line that lands, because every good tradesperson
> believes it about themselves and is usually right.

---

## Email 3: short and direct

**Subject:** {{business}} website

Hi,

I made {{business}} a website. It took me a couple of hours and it is free to
look at: {{site}}

If you want it live, reply and I will walk you through it. If not, ignore this
and I will not chase you.

{{your_name}}

> **Why this works.** Some owners will not read past two lines. This one respects
> that. Use it when the scrape suggests a smaller operation, a sole trader, or a
> mobile number rather than a landline.
