# 08. Payment failed

**Send:** the day the payment bounces.
**Goal:** fix the card, keep the relationship. This is almost never them leaving.

---

**Subject:** Card didn't go through

Hi [First name],

Your monthly payment didn't go through this month, which is usually just an
expired card.

Here's the link to update it:

[payment link]

Nothing's changed on your end, the site's still live and your enquiries are still
coming through. Just wanted to flag it before it went another month.

[Your name]

---

## If there is still nothing after a week

**Subject:** Everything alright?

Hi [First name],

Still nothing through on the card, so I wanted to check in properly rather than
keep sending you payment links.

If it's just been a busy fortnight, here's the link: [payment link]

If something's changed and you want to talk about the arrangement, ring me. I'd
rather sort it out than have it go quiet.

[Your name]

---

## Notes

- The first email assumes it is a card problem, because nine times out of ten it
  is. Never open with a suggestion that they are avoiding you.
- Do not switch anything off. Taking a client's site down over one failed payment
  turns a card expiry into a public argument and a bad review.
- If the second email gets nothing, ring. Then use `09-win-back.md`.
- ⚠️ Whatever your agreement says about suspension, tell them before you act on
  it, not after.
