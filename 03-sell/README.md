# 3. Sell

Everything here has one goal. **Get them on a call.**

## The three shapes of outreach

Pick one per lead. All three end the same way.

1. **A proposal with their site embedded**, then book a call
2. **A video plus that proposal**, then book a call
3. **A video alone**, then book a call

⚠️ **Never a form.** A busy owner will not answer questions for a stranger they
have not decided to trust. The only form anybody fills in is onboarding, and that
goes out after they pay.

## The tools

| Tool | When |
|---|---|
| `tools/instant-builder` | Builds each lead a real site and proposal in about 45 seconds. This is the cold outreach hook |
| `tools/vsl-page` | Your evergreen video page, for when you would rather not do a live call for every lead |
| `tools/sales-proposal` | What you put on screen during the call. Contract signed on the page |

## The call

The script is `scripts/phone/discovery-call.md`. The short version:

1. Agree up front that a yes or a no today are both fine
2. Ask six questions about how they get work, and shut up
3. Say their answers back to them and get a yes
4. Walk the growth equation, tying each lever to what they told you
5. Say the price and stop talking
6. Handle the real objection, not the wrapper
7. Take payment and the signature on the call

⚠️ **Never promise a result.** No doubling their calls. You sell the mechanism and
a standard of work, and you keep going until they are happy. That is what the
agreement says and it is what keeps you out of trouble.

## The money

Set your prices in the proposal's seller panel, and your payment links in Stripe.
See `00-setup/10-stripe.md`.

⚠️ Work starts after the money clears, not before. The non-refundable clause only
protects you if that order is kept.
