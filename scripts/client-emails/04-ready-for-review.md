# 04. Ready for review

**Send:** when the preview is up and you have checked it yourself.
**Goal:** useful feedback in one round, not five.

---

**Subject:** Have a look at this

Hi [First name],

Here it is:

[preview link]

Have a proper look on your phone as well as a computer, that's where most of your
customers will see it.

Three things worth checking specifically:

1. Your services and the areas you cover, have I got them right
2. Your phone number and opening hours
3. The photos, is there anything on there you'd rather wasn't

Send me everything you want changed in one go and I'll do it in one pass. If
it's easier to just ring me and talk through it, do that instead.

[Your name]

---

## Notes

- Never send this before you have checked it yourself at phone width and
  submitted the enquiry form. If they find something broken in the first
  thirty seconds you spend the rest of the project defending the work.
- Asking for changes "in one go" is what keeps this to one round. Without that
  line you get seven separate emails over four days.
- The three specific checks stop them reviewing the colour of a button and
  missing that a service is wrong.
