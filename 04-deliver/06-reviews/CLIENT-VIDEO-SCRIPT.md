# The video your client records

Thirty to forty five seconds, filmed on their phone, for the thank-you page every
customer lands on after a finished job. It powers both the review ask and the
referral program, so it is the same video for line 05 and line 06.

Send them this page. It is written for them, not for you.

---

## For the business owner

You do not need a camera, a microphone, or a good day. Hold your phone up, stand
somewhere with your van or your work behind you, and talk like you would to a
customer on the doorstep.

Film it in one take. If you fluff a word, keep going. Nobody trusts a polished
one.

### Say roughly this

> Hi, it's [name] from [business]. Thanks very much for having us out, it was
> good to meet you.
>
> If you were happy with the job, the biggest favour you could do us is leave a
> quick Google review. It genuinely helps people find us, and we're a small
> outfit so it makes a real difference.
>
> And if you know anyone who needs [the work you do], send them our way. There's
> a [reward] in it for you if they book, and they'll get [discount] off.
>
> Thanks again, and give us a shout if you need anything.

### Three things that make it work

1. **Say their trade, not "our services".** "If you know anyone who needs a
   boiler sorting" beats "if you require our services".
2. **Look at the lens, not at yourself on the screen.** It is the one thing that
   makes a phone video feel like eye contact.
3. **Do not read it.** Read it twice, put the phone up, say it in your own words.

### What not to do

- Do not film in the van with the engine running
- Do not do it at the end of a bad day
- Do not ask for a five star review specifically. Ask for an honest one

---

## For the person building the site

- Ask for this during onboarding, in the same message as the photos. Chasing it
  later is much harder than getting it in the first week.
- Under a minute. Over that and nobody finishes it.
- Filmed upright on a phone is correct. Do not ask them for landscape.
- If they will not do it, the page still works without it. A written thank you
  from the owner is a reasonable substitute and better than an empty player.

⚠️ The reward and the discount they say out loud must match what you have set in
the CRM. If the video says fifty and the page says twenty five, that is an
argument with a customer that your client has to have.

⚠️ Never script them into promising a result or a guarantee. They are thanking
somebody, not advertising.
