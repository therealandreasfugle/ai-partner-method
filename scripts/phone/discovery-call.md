# The call that closes

Twenty to thirty minutes. Runs on a call, with the proposal open in front of you.

**The single biggest mistake is walking them through the website.** They are not
buying a website. They are buying more work, and a way to stop losing the jobs
they already nearly had. Talk about their business for two thirds of this call.

---

## 1. Set the frame (1 minute)

> Thanks for making the time. Here is what I want to do. I will ask you a few
> things about how you get work at the moment, because I do not want to pitch you
> something you do not need. Then I will show you what I built and what it costs,
> and you can tell me yes or no today. Either is fine. Sound alright?

Getting "yes or no today is fine" agreed at the start is what stops the "let me
think about it" ending.

---

## 2. Where does the work come from (5 minutes)

Ask these and shut up. Write down their exact words, you will use them later.

1. How do people find you at the moment?
2. Roughly how much of your work is word of mouth?
3. What happens when somebody rings and you are on a job?
4. When somebody fills in a form or messages you, how quickly do you usually get
   back to them?
5. Do you ever ask customers for a Google review?
6. When was the last quiet month, and what did you do about it?

> **Question 3 and 4 are the ones that sell this.** Almost every owner admits
> they miss calls and get back to people late. That admission, in their words, is
> the whole reason the system is worth money. You are not going to argue them
> into it, you are going to let them tell you.

---

## 3. Say it back (2 minutes)

> So if I have got this right: most of your work comes from people who already
> know you, you are losing calls when you are on a job, and you have never really
> asked anyone for a review even though you have got plenty of happy customers.
>
> Is that fair?

Wait for the yes. **Do not move on without it.** That yes is the sale.

---

## 4. Show them (8 minutes)

Now open the proposal and walk the growth equation, not the design.

- Traffic: getting found by people who do not know your name yet
- Trust: the five seconds they spend deciding you are safe
- Conversion: the enquiry actually reaching you, fast, every time
- Repeat and referral: the customers you already have sending you the next ones

Tie each one to something they said in step 2. If they told you they miss calls,
spend the time on conversion and the instant alert. If they told you it is all
word of mouth, spend it on referral.

Then show the site. Two minutes, not ten.

---

## 5. Price (2 minutes)

Say the number and then stop talking. The first person to speak after a price
loses.

> The build is {{setup_fee}} to get everything live, and then it is
> {{monthly}} a month to keep it running, which covers the hosting, the edits,
> the reporting and the follow up system.

Silence. However long it takes.

---

## 6. Handling the four objections

**"It is too expensive."**
> Compared to what it brings in, or compared to what you expected? Those are
> different problems and I can help with one of them.

**"I need to think about it."**
> Course. What specifically do you want to think about? Usually it is the money,
> the timing, or whether it will actually work. Which one is it?

Then handle the real one. "I need to think about it" is never the objection, it
is the wrapper.

**"I need to speak to my wife or my business partner."**
> Completely fair. When are you speaking to them? Can I be on that call, or shall
> I send you something you can show them?

**"How do I know it will work?"**
> You do not, and I am not going to promise you a number I cannot control. What I
> will do is keep working on it until it is right, which is in the agreement.
> What I can tell you is what it fixes: you stop missing enquiries, and people
> looking for a {{trade}} in {{town}} can actually find you.

> ⚠️ **Never promise a result.** No "this will double your calls". You cannot
> control their market and a promise you cannot keep is how you end up with a
> chargeback. Sell the mechanism and the standard of work.

---

## 7. Close

> Happy to get started?

If yes, take payment on the call and get the agreement signed on the call. A
signature tomorrow is a signature that does not happen.

If no, ask what would need to be true, book the follow up before you hang up, and
put it in the CRM.
