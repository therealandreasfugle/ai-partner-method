# 6. Your own credibility

**You are selling websites. Not having one is the loudest thing about you.**

This folder is a checklist rather than a tool, and it is the most commonly skipped
part of this whole business.

## The three things, in order

**1. Your own site.** Use one of the 20 templates. Give yourself an afternoon,
not a week. It needs: what you do, who for, a few examples, and a way to book a
call. That is it. A perfect site you never launch is worth nothing.

**2. Proof you can show.** You are allowed to show the demo builds in this repo as
examples of the work you deliver. Once you have real clients, replace them with
those. Screenshots of real sites you built beat any claim you can write.

**3. Somewhere they can check you exist.** A Google Business Profile of your own,
one social profile you actually post on, and a real email address on your own
domain. An owner will look you up before a call. Give them something to find.

## What not to do

⚠️ **Never invent a testimonial, a client count, or a case study result.** You are
about to spend a year telling local businesses that honesty beats marketing
gloss. Doing the opposite on your own site is both wrong and the easiest thing in
the world to get caught doing.

⚠️ Do not claim clients you do not have. "I build sites for local businesses" is
true on day one. "Trusted by 50 businesses" is not, and one wrong question on a
call ends the deal.

## The honest version of your first pitch

You do not need years of history. You need one thing they cannot argue with: you
already built them a site before you called. That is the proof. Everything else
is decoration.
