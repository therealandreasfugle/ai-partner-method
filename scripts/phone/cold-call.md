# Cold call and voicemail

Used with the power dialler in the CRM. The dialler goes through your own phone,
so there is nothing to set up and nothing billed to you.

## Before you dial

- **Call between 8am and 10am, or after 4pm.** Trades are on a job in the middle
  of the day and will not thank you for interrupting it.
- Have their demo site open. If they say "send it over" you send it while you are
  still on the phone, not later.
- Log every outcome in the CRM the moment you hang up. The follow up you do not
  log is the follow up you do not make.

---

## The opener

> Hi, is that {{business}}?
>
> Good. My name is {{your_name}}, I am not selling you anything on this call, I
> just built you something and I wanted to check I should send it to you rather
> than binning it. Have you got twenty seconds?

Stop talking. Let them answer.

> **Why it works.** "I am not selling you anything on this call" is true, because
> you are not, you are getting permission to send a link. It disarms the reflex
> they use on every other cold call. "Twenty seconds" is a smaller ask than "two
> minutes" and you must honour it.

---

## If they say go on

> I build websites for {{trade}} businesses around {{town}}. I built one for
> {{business}} already, it is finished, you can look at it today.
>
> I am not asking you to buy it. I would just rather you saw it than deleted an
> email about it. What is the best address to send it to?

Get the email. That is the entire goal of the call. **Do not pitch further.** You
have their attention and their address, which is more than the last ten people
who called them got.

---

## If they say what does it cost

> Genuinely depends on what you need, and I would be making a number up if I
> answered that before knowing how you get your work now.
>
> Look at the site first, it is free and it is already built. If you like it we
> will have that conversation properly.

---

## If they say we already have a website

> Most people I call do. Mine is not really about having one, it is about whether
> the people looking for a {{trade}} in {{town}} ever get to yours.
>
> Have a look at what I built and tell me if it is better than what you have got.
> If it is not, I will leave you alone.

> Never say their site is bad. Ask them to compare. They will do the criticising
> for you, and then it is their conclusion instead of your insult.

---

## If they say we are too busy for more work

> Fair enough, that is a good problem.
>
> Can I send it anyway? Costs you nothing and quiet months turn up eventually.
> You will have it when you want it.

---

## If they say send me an email

They are brushing you off. Take it anyway, but pin it down.

> Will do. What address, and can I check back with you on Thursday?

A named day converts far better than "I will follow up".

---

## Voicemail

Leave one. Keep it under fifteen seconds. Never leave a second one on the same
number in the same week.

> Hi, it is {{your_name}}. I build websites for {{trade}} businesses around
> {{town}} and I have already built one for {{business}}, so there is nothing to
> buy, I just want to send it to you. My number is {{your_phone}}. If it is
> easier, I will try you again in a couple of days.

> **Why it works.** It gives a reason for the call that is not a pitch, it names
> a thing that already exists, and it tells them you will call again so the next
> call is expected rather than cold.
