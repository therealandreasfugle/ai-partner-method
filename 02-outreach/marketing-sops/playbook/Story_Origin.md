## Story & Origin

### Direct Personal Relatability Through Struggle & Origin

Lead with humble origin stories and sacrifice—emphasize where you came from (lack, struggle, immigrant background) rather than where you are now to build authentic connection and differentiate from lifestyle-flexers. A personal brand showcases who you are, your unique traits, what you've been through, and what you're going through now, making it relatable to your audience.

> "I want my audience to know me for someone that that was raised with very little to no money but with the most humble and modest pairings that you could ever have"

> "A personal brand is what signifies your personality, is what showcases who you are, your unique traits, what you've been through, what you're going through at the moment, making it relatable to your audience."

### Vulnerability & Personal Struggles as Connection Drivers

Share vulnerable personal stories—struggles, near-poverty, embarrassment, moments on the brink—in your content to build know-and-like factor and make your audience feel they can relate to your journey. When people relate to you deeply, they will buy anything you offer regardless of what it is.

> "you have to showcase what your story is, what your past history is, what your struggles, what your relatability factor. Essentially, you portraying yourself on social media and you explain to yourself your story, your come up, your values and everything. Why? So then people can relate to you as much as possible. And when people relate to you, it doesn't matter what you offer. They're going to buy anything that you offer them just because they like you so much."

> "If you talk about how you were getting sales, but then you got into how to get sales and for the first six to eight months you had so much stuff. You were on the brim of your bed.. Imagine you're talking about your story, of what you went through, and your audience that's looking at your video are going through the exact thing right now. Imagine the power you're now hold because that lit ability is so strong, it's so powerful."
> "Wednesday, share a personal story that will inspire your audience. This could be future goals, a story about when you could be wrist or any personal journey. Okay. This is being vulnerable. It's getting your audience to know and like you."

### Real-World Impact Over Lifestyle Flexing

Use authentic personal stories about providing for family and solving real problems to demonstrate genuine impact and values-alignment with prospects, not flashy wealth displays. Humble origin stories and the values behind your decisions build more trust than lifestyle flexing.

> "I remember our AC broke $10,000, $10,000 to fix it. Did it flinch? Why? Because this is what it's supposed to be for. That's why I'm not really a flashy guy. All this, it's supposed to be able to help my family."

### Progression Stories & Mirror Positioning

Tell your origin story showing monthly growth from low starting point (e.g., $500 month 1) to high-end results (e.g., $60K month) so prospects see you were once in their position and understand that investment in mentorship or your mechanism was the turning point. Share ambition, struggles, and transformation journey in relatability content so people follow your personal journey, not just your information.

> "This shows people that he was at one point just like them, right. He wasn't always making $60,000 a month, $100,000 a month, whatever it is now. He was at one point only making $500 a month and he had $40 to start."

> "Stories sell over here. I had a real talking about how I went from a DM setter to a consultant or to a growth operator managing companies and how I was able to do so off a real 6.1 K because people heard the ambition I had. People heard the struggles I had to go through."

### Indirect Relatability Through Client Transformations

Use client transformations where their past situation mirrors your audience's current situation, then show how your mechanism got them results. This leverages social proof while maintaining relatability across multiple audience segments.

> "Indirect relatability is when I'm talking about my mentee, Gabe or Daniel or Adam or whoever or Jerry, whoever it might be. And their past situation is your current situation."
