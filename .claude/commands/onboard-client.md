A client has paid. Get them from payment to a built site with nothing dropped.

**Do these in order:**

1. **Confirm the money actually cleared.** Not "they said they would pay". Work
   starts after it lands, because the agreement's non-refundable clause only
   protects the user if that order is kept.
2. **Send the onboarding form** at `04-deliver/client-onboarding`. Same day.
   Write the email for the user, short, warm, one link, and say it takes about
   ten minutes.
3. **Chase it if it does not come back in two days.** Not with a nag, with an
   offer to fill it in together on a ten minute call. Most stalled builds are a
   form nobody filled in.
4. **When the answers arrive**, read them and flag anything missing that will
   block the build: no logo, no photos, no Google review link, no Business
   Profile access.
5. **Run `/build-site`.**
6. **Set up the rest of what they bought**, using the folders in `04-deliver`:
   lead alerts and Slack, the chatbot, SEO, GA4, the review engine and the
   referral page.

⚠️ The Google review link and the Business Profile access are the two things
clients most often skip, and the review engine cannot run without them. Chase
those specifically.

⚠️ Do not promise a launch date until the form is back. The clock starts when
they give you what you need, and saying so up front prevents the most common
argument in this business.
