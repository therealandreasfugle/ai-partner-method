# Video 15: Deliver line 10, GA4 analytics

**Length:** 4 minutes. **Value on the proposal:** $300.
**Source:** `04-deliver/10-analytics`.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> Seeing what is actually happening. Small line item, but it is the one that
> justifies the monthly fee every single month, so do not treat it as an
> afterthought.

---

## SCREEN

1. Create a property at https://analytics.google.com. One per client, never
   shared.
2. Copy the measurement ID. It looks like G dash and then a string of characters.
3. Add it to their site config.
4. Load the live site yourself and confirm you show up in Realtime.

TALK ABOUT while Realtime loads:
- That last step is the whole verification. If you can see yourself on the site in
  real time, it is installed correctly.

---

## SCREEN: the only four numbers that matter

Put these on screen. This is the part of the video worth watching.

1. How many people found the site, and whether that went up.
2. How many enquired. This is the only number that turns into money.
3. Where they came from. Mostly Google versus everything else.
4. Which pages they actually read, which tells you what to build more of.

**SAY THIS:**

> Do not send a client a link to a GA4 dashboard. They will open it once, feel
> stupid, and never open it again. Send them those four numbers written out in
> plain English. Everything else in there is for people who enjoy GA4.

---

## THE ONE THING

Two halves, both about timing and ownership.

**SAY THIS:**

> Set this up on day one, not in month three. Analytics cannot tell you anything
> about traffic from before you installed it, and that first month is the baseline
> every future report gets compared against. Miss it and you have nothing to show
> improvement against for a year.
>
> And put the property in their Google account, or at the very least make them an
> admin on it. Same rule as the domain. If it lives only in your account and they
> leave, they lose their entire history, and that is a genuinely bad thing to do
> to somebody.
