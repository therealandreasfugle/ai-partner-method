# 04. SEO foundation, schema and AEO

**What the client is paying for:** being found by people who do not know their
name yet. Value on the proposal: $1,200.

**Where the system lives:** built into the website factory,
`tools/website-template` and its `personalize.py`.

## What is already automatic

You do not build any of this by hand. It comes out of the build:

- **Structured data** so Google understands the business: Organization,
  LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, per-location and breadcrumbs
- **A page per service area**, which is the largest part of ranking locally
- **A page per service**
- **sitemap.xml and robots.txt**
- **Speed**, gated so the site passes Core Web Vitals

## What AEO actually means

People increasingly ask an AI assistant for a local business instead of scrolling
Google. Whether the client gets named depends on the site being readable and
quotable. The FAQ structured data is what does that, and it is crawlable without
JavaScript, which the visible accordion is not.

⚠️ Do not promise a client that an AI will recommend them. You are structuring the
site to be readable and quotable. That is the honest claim and it is enough.

## You MUST customise

- Every service area they actually cover, from their form. Do not invent towns
  they do not serve, it is the fastest way to rank for work they cannot do
- Their real services
- The FAQ answers, from what their customers actually ask

## Known gap

Per-page titles and descriptions currently inherit the homepage. Set the homepage
ones well, and if a client cares about a specific service page, set it by hand.
