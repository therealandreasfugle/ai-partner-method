# Video 6: Deliver line 01, the website build

**Length:** 4 minutes. **Value on the proposal:** $4,500.
**Source:** `04-deliver/01-website`. **Systems:** `tools/website-templates`,
`tools/website-template`.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> This is the biggest line on the proposal and the one that takes the least time,
> because you are not designing anything. You are picking one of twenty finished
> sites and filling in their real details.

---

## SCREEN

1. Open their onboarding form answers side by side with the gallery.
2. Open `tools/website-templates/templates`. Pick the closest one. Match the trade
   first, then the feel they asked for on the form.
3. In Claude Code:

```
/build-site
```

4. Show it reading the form answers and filling in services, service areas, hours,
   phone, reviews and photos.
5. Show it deploying to a Vercel project of its own.

TALK ABOUT while it builds:
- One project per client. Never share a project between two clients.
- Everything it fills in comes from their form. If a field was blank on the form,
  it stays blank rather than getting invented.

---

## YOU MUST CUSTOMISE

- Every service and service area, from their form
- Phone, email, address, hours
- Their real photos
- Their real reviews

**SAY THIS:**

> Their own photos, always. Stock photos of somebody else's roof is the fastest
> way to look like every other cheap website in their town. If they have nothing,
> tell them to walk round a finished job with their phone for ten minutes. That
> beats anything you can buy.

---

## CHECK IT

Do all four on camera:

- Open the live URL at desktop width and at phone width
- Submit the enquiry form yourself and watch the alert email arrive
- Click every link
- Search the page for any leftover template text

---

## THE ONE THING

**SAY THIS:**

> Never invent a review, a rating, a year founded, a customer count or an
> accreditation. If the form left it blank, ask them or leave it off the site.
> A made up five star review on a real business is their problem, not just yours,
> and it is the one mistake that ends the relationship on day one.

---

## CLOSE

> That is the site. Next, somewhere for the leads it produces to actually live.
