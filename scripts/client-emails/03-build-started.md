# 03. Build started

**Send:** the day their answers arrive.
**Goal:** they know what happens, when, and that nothing is waiting on them.

---

**Subject:** Got everything, starting on it

Hi [First name],

Got your answers, thanks. That's everything I need.

Here's the plan:

- I'll have a first version for you to look at by [day]
- You'll get a private link, not the live site, so nothing is public yet
- You tell me what to change, I change it
- Then we point your web address at it and you're live

Nothing needed from you until that link lands.

One thing I noticed: [the one gap from their form, or delete this line]. Not
urgent, but if you can dig it out this week it'll save us a round.

[Your name]

---

## Notes

- Give a real day. "Next week" gets you chased. A named day does not.
- The gap line is where you ask for the thing they left blank. One thing, not a
  list. The most common ones are the logo, decent photos, and the Google review
  link.
- If they gave you everything, delete that paragraph rather than inventing a
  request.
