# Pre-Call Sequence (4 emails)

**Goal:** Warm up booked leads so they show up to the call and are primed to buy.

**Trigger:** Lead books a call (e.g. via Calendly, Typeform-to-call, etc.).

**Total send window:** From booking until 2 hours before the call.

**Recommended framework:** PAS (audience knows the pain — they just booked a call to fix it).

Replace every `[bracketed placeholder]` with content from your brief. Lines marked `>>>` are notes for you, not part of the email.

---

## Email 1 — Confirmation

**Send:** Immediately after booking
**Framework:** Direct / acknowledgment
**Goal:** Lock in expectation, prevent "did this go through?" anxiety.

```
Subject: [First Name], you're confirmed for [Day] at [Time]

Hey [First Name],

Your call is locked in for [Date] at [Time].

Here's what we'll cover:
- [Topic 1 — specific, from brief]
- [Topic 2]
- [Topic 3]

Before we chat, think about:
- [Question 1 — the one that surfaces their pain]
- [Question 2 — the one that surfaces their goal]

Talk soon,
[Sender Name]

P.S. Add this to your calendar: [Calendar Link]
```

>>> Why this works: They just booked. They want immediate confirmation that the system processed it. Add the prep questions — leads who answer in their head before the call show up at 90%+ rates.

---

## Email 2 — Social Proof

**Send:** 24 hours before the call
**Framework:** BAB (Before / After / Bridge)
**Goal:** Reduce risk, raise belief.

```
Subject: [Specific Result] — [Client Name]'s story

Hey [First Name],

[Client Name] was exactly where you are right now.

[2–3 sentences about their before state — be specific. Industry, size, struggle. From brief's proof_points.]

Then [what happened — the turning point. One sentence.]

Result: [Specific, measurable outcome with a number. Not "amazing" or "huge".]

This is exactly what we'll map out on your call [Day].

[Calendar reminder: Date / Time / Link if needed]

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: 24 hours out, the lead's enthusiasm is fading. A real, specific case study re-anchors why they booked. Numbers > adjectives every time.

---

## Email 3 — Value Add

**Send:** 12 hours before the call
**Framework:** Educational / authority
**Goal:** Position as the expert before they show up.

```
Subject: Before your call

Hey [First Name],

Quick thing before we talk tomorrow.

Most [audience] who come to me with [core_pain] try to fix it by [common-but-wrong approach]. That's why it's not working.

The real shift is [the actual insight — the thing your offer delivers, in one sentence].

Here's a [resource — short article, 5-min video, case study breakdown] that walks through it: [Link]

Watch it before our call — it'll save us 15 minutes and let us go deeper.

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: They feel like they're getting value before they've paid anything. The "watch this" ask is small, but it pre-loads the conversation — by the time you're on the call, they already believe you know what you're talking about.

>>> Don't have a resource yet? Skip this email. Better than sending a generic "tip" that doesn't land.

---

## Email 4 — Final Reminder

**Send:** 2 hours before the call
**Framework:** Direct
**Goal:** Reduce no-shows.

```
Subject: Re: your [Time] call

Hey [First Name],

Quick reminder — we're on at [Time] (your timezone).

Calendar link if you need it: [Link]

A couple things to have ready:
- [Specific item 1 — e.g. their current numbers, recent campaign data, etc.]
- [Specific item 2]

See you in 2.

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: 2-hour reminder catches the people who put the call on their calendar but forgot. The "have ready" list does double duty: shows up rates go up because they're now mentally preparing.

## Customization checklist

Before loading these into your ESP:

- [ ] Replace **all** `[brackets]` with brief content.
- [ ] Confirm `{{first_name}}` syntax matches your ESP's tokens.
- [ ] Confirm calendar link goes to the *right* calendar.
- [ ] Trigger logic in ESP: send #1 immediately, #2 at booking_time - 24h, #3 at booking_time - 12h, #4 at booking_time - 2h.
- [ ] If the call is rescheduled, the ESP should reset the schedule — test this.
- [ ] Email 2's case study uses real client data only. If no real story exists, skip Email 2 and renumber.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                         | Fix                                                  |
|-------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Sending all 4 emails on the same day            | Stagger by trigger time, not by drip days.           |
| Generic "looking forward!" Email #4             | Add the "have ready" list — it's the show-rate lift. |
| Case study from a different niche               | Use a story the lead can see themselves in.          |
| Email #3 with a 30-min video                    | Too much. Keep the resource ≤5 minutes.              |
| No P.S. on Email #1                             | The calendar link in P.S. is the highest-clicked link in this whole sequence. |
