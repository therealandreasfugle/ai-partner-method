# Client emails

Every email you send a paying client, from the moment their money clears to the
month they nearly leave.

These are different from `../cold-email`. Those are for strangers and they are
written to get a call. These are for someone who has already paid you, and they
are written to keep them.

## The order they get used

| # | Email | When |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Payment received](01-payment-received.md) | The hour the money clears |
| 02 | [Chase the form](02-chase-the-form.md) | Two days later if it has not come back |
| 03 | [Build started](03-build-started.md) | The day their answers arrive |
| 04 | [Ready for review](04-ready-for-review.md) | When the preview link is up |
| 05 | [Launch day](05-launch-day.md) | The day it goes live on their domain |
| 06 | [Monthly report](06-monthly-report.md) | Same date every month, forever |
| 07 | [Edit acknowledged](07-edit-acknowledged.md) | Within the hour, every time |
| 08 | [Payment failed](08-payment-failed.md) | When a monthly payment bounces |
| 09 | [Win back](09-win-back.md) | Two weeks after they cancel |
| 10 | [Seasonal check in](10-seasonal.md) | Once or twice a year, before they need you |

## How to use them

Replace anything in square brackets. Never send one with a bracket left in it.

Keep them short. A local business owner reads email on a phone between jobs. If
it does not fit on one screen, it does not get read.

Write like a person. No "I hope this email finds you well", no "please do not
hesitate", no "circling back". You are a supplier they like, not a corporation.

## The two that actually matter

**06, the monthly report.** Send it every month whether or not they ask. This
single email is the difference between a client who stays three years and one who
cancels in month four. It is not optional and it is not skippable in a quiet
month.

**07, the edit acknowledgement.** Reply within the hour, even if you cannot do
the work for two days. A same-day reply to "can you change the phone number" buys
more loyalty than any report you will ever send.

⚠️ Never promise a result in any of these. No traffic numbers, no lead counts, no
rankings. You report what happened and what you did. That is what the agreement
says and it is what keeps you out of an argument you cannot win.
