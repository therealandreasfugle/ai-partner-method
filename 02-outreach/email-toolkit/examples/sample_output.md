## What to notice in this sample

1. **Specific, not generic.** Every email references the audience's actual life — energy crash at 3 pm, family vacation photos, "65 at 42." That specificity is from the brief, not from the AI's imagination.

2. **Tone matches the brief.** "Direct, confident, no-fluff, slightly contrarian" shows up in every email — *"stop negotiating with yourself,"* *"the program has to fit your life, not the other way around,"* *"I'm not going to coddle you."* No exclamation marks, no hype words.

3. **One CTA per email.** All four emails point to the same destination (the booked call). No multiple links pulling attention.

4. **Real proof points.** When Email 2 references results, it's grounded in "8 years of coaching guys exactly like you" and structural insights — not invented testimonials.

5. **Subject lines pass the rules.** All are 5–7 words, lowercase-friendly, no spam triggers, action-relevant.

6. **The 4 pillars in Email 3 match the actual program.** Custom programming, 2x calls, nutrition fit, recovery — all specific. If your brief lists 4 program pillars, this email pulls them in directly.

This is the bar. When you (or Claude) produces an output, hold it against this sample.
