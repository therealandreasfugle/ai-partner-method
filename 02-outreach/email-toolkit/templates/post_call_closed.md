# Post-Call: Closed (2 emails)

**Goal:** Onboard new clients smoothly. Reduce buyer's remorse and refunds.

**Trigger:** Lead pays / signs / closes on the call.

**Recommended framework:** Direct / welcoming. No selling needed — they already bought.

---

## Email 1 — Welcome

**Send:** Immediately after the close
**Goal:** Reinforce the decision, set the next 24-hour expectation.

```
Subject: Welcome to [Program Name]

Hey [First Name],

Welcome in. You just made a great call.

Here's what happens next:

1. [Specific step 1 — e.g. "You'll get an onboarding link from [name] within 30 minutes."]
2. [Specific step 2 — e.g. "Tomorrow at 10am, your kickoff call. Calendar invite is on the way."]
3. [Specific step 3 — e.g. "Inside the portal, watch the 'Start Here' video first."]

The team is locked in for you. We're going to make this work.

Talk soon,
[Sender Name]

P.S. If anything's unclear in the first 24 hours — just reply to this email. I read every one.
```

>>> Why this works: The first hour after closing is when buyer's remorse spikes. A specific, confident "here's what happens next" replaces uncertainty with momentum. The P.S. matters — it tells them they're not abandoned to a support queue.

---

## Email 2 — Onboarding & Access

**Send:** Day 1 (~12 hours after Email 1)
**Goal:** Give them everything they need to start, without overwhelming.

```
Subject: Your [Program] access — start here

Hey [First Name],

Everything you need to get started:

→ Login: [URL]
→ Username: [their email]
→ Password reset link: [URL]

The first thing to do (takes 10 minutes):
[Single specific action — e.g. "Watch the 'Welcome' video and fill out your goals form. That feeds into the personalized roadmap your coach builds for you."]

Your first [coaching call / module / check-in] is on [Day, Time]. Calendar invite already in your inbox.

Quick housekeeping:
- Slack channel invite is in your inbox too (subject: "[Slack workspace name] invite")
- Save [support@email.com] to your contacts so we don't end up in spam
- Office hours every [Day] at [Time] if you want to drop in

Excited to have you.

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: Specific. Sequenced. Tells them what to do *first* (not all 47 things at once). The "save to contacts" line is a deliverability pro move — keeps your follow-ups out of spam during the most engaged 30 days of their relationship with you.

---

## Customization checklist

- [ ] Replace **all** `[brackets]` and ensure URLs are real, not placeholders.
- [ ] Confirm login system actually sends a password reset link (test on your own email first).
- [ ] Calendar invite goes out automatically, OR Email 2 explicitly tells them to expect it.
- [ ] Slack/community channel invite is automated, not manual (manual = you forget = bad first impression).
- [ ] Reply-to on Email 1 goes to a human, not a no-reply.
- [ ] Set expectations for first call timing accurately. If it's 3 days out, say "3 days" — don't say "tomorrow" and let them down.

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                         | Fix                                                       |
|-------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Generic "thanks for your purchase!"             | Be specific. Use the program name, the next step, the human contact. |
| 12 links in Email 2                             | One primary action. Other links are secondary.            |
| Sending Email 2 immediately after Email 1       | Spaced ~12h apart. Otherwise feels like a flood.          |
| No-reply email address                          | Always reply-able. The first reply is gold for retention.  |
| Selling the upsell in Email 2                   | Wait 7+ days. Selling on Day 1 = refund spike.            |
