### Hard Deadlines & Scarcity

Use urgency and action-forcing language tied to a real, approaching deadline—tell prospects the exact number of remaining spots and that you won't reopen membership.

> I'd get in sooner than later cuz we got about 40-ish slots left for that 250 cap to be hit and we will not go above that. We'll waitlist everybody beyond it.

Create FOMO by being transparent about your progress toward the hard cap—mention the exact pace of new members joining so prospects feel the deadline approaching.

> We're getting about 20 to 25 people a month joining in. We raised the price a bit, which dramatically improved the quality of member.. We started pacing at about 10 to 15 people a month. Our slowest months have been about eight people a month.

### Moving Beyond Single-Event Conversion

Include CTAs at three points in YouTube videos: at 30% through (for those who want early value), at 100% at the end (for committed viewers), and in description with TypeForm/calendar links for capture, avoiding single upfront CTA.

> At the end of every single video they have a CTA to book a call besides the building and relationship with the personal ones which you want to do is you want to redirect them to another video that will be educational.

Use story highlights with CTAs strategically—don't exhaust your audience with CTAs everywhere; balance educational/lifestyle content with strategic call-to-action sequences that move prospects to YouTube or DMs.

### Multi-Platform CTA Strategy

End every content piece (reels, stories, carousels) with a direct CTA tied to your conversion goal—either 'DM me for [free resource],' 'Book a call,' 'Reply in comments,' or 'Check the link in bio'—matched to the funnel stage (top/middle/bottom).

> There are various different ways you could directly engage with a prospect, whether it's through a poll, CTA for lean magnets, CTA to book a call, Q and A, etc. Message me on Instagram if you'd like, book a call with me.

Use a rotating Instagram Stories CTA structure to prevent audience fatigue: Monday (client results + direct DM CTA), Tuesday (pain point content), Wednesday (personal story), Thursday (your wins + soft CTA), Friday-Sunday (flexible content).

> Monday post a client result with a direct call to action to DM you. Tuesday. Provide value and help them understand why they haven't achieved their goal yet. Wednesday, share a personal story that will inspire your audience. Thursday, posting your own wins, show payments, screenshots, personal achievement.
