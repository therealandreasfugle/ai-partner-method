# Booked Call: High-Ticket (4 emails)

**Goal:** Reinforce the call commitment for $5k+ programs. Show rate + close rate matter more than open rate.

**Trigger:** Lead books a discovery / strategy call for a high-ticket offer.

**Recommended framework:** Mix — direct (#1), educational authority (#2), structural breakdown (#3), urgency/scarcity (#4).

**When to use this vs. the standard pre-call sequence:**
- **Standard pre-call** (`pre_call_sequence.md`): under $3k offers, transactional sales calls.
- **High-ticket** (this file): $5k+ offers where the lead needs to be sold on *the program's philosophy* before the call, not just the call itself.

---

## Email 1 — Confirmation + Framing

**Send:** Immediately after booking
**Goal:** Lock in the call and frame what the call means.

```
Subject: Your [Program Name] call is locked in

Hey [First Name],

I just saw your name come up on my calendar.

Let me be straight with you — what you just did separates you from 95% of [audience] who are destined to fail.

Most [audience] are stuck in an endless cycle of [pain point 1], [pain point 2], and [pain point 3].

But you booked this call. That single action separates you from the 95%.

It means you want to [desire 1].
You want to [desire 2].
You want [desire 3].

That's exactly what happens inside [Program Name].

On your call, we'll see if you're ready to join a small group of [audience] who've stopped [bad behavior] and started [good behavior].

One last thing: if you keep doing what you're doing now, where will you be in 6 months?

Time to change that story.

— [Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: The "95% / 5%" frame is psychologically powerful — they self-identify as the 5%, raising the stakes of the call. The closing question forces a future-pacing exercise. Don't soften this — the bluntness is the point.

---

## Email 2 — Value Education

**Send:** 24h before the call
**Framework:** Authority through contrarian insight
**Goal:** Position you as the expert before they show up.

```
Subject: What separates the top 1%

Hey [First Name],

Before your call tomorrow, I need to share something most "[industry] gurus" will never tell you.

[Industry] is deliberately designed to [painful systemic truth]. That's not your fault — it's the design.

But the uncomfortable truth: the real edge isn't [common misconception]. It's [actual insight].

After [X years] in this space, I've watched the same pattern destroy [audience]:

→ [Mistake 1 — specific]
→ [Mistake 2]
→ [Mistake 3]

Here's what separates the top 1% from everyone else: [Key insight in one sentence.]

[Program Name] isn't about [surface-level thing competitors offer]. It's about [deeper structural change].

My team will walk you through exactly how that works on your call.

— [Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: Authority comes from naming what others won't. The contrarian frame ("what gurus will never tell you") makes the reader lean in. The 3 mistakes pre-handle 80% of objections — by the time they're on the call, those concerns are already addressed.

---

## Email 3 — How It Works

**Send:** Morning of the call (or evening before, if call is in morning)
**Framework:** Structural breakdown
**Goal:** Pre-sell the program's mechanism so the call is about *fit*, not *what*.

```
Subject: Exactly how [Program Name] works

Hey [First Name],

Here's why [Program Name] is structurally different from anything else you've tried:

1. [Pillar 1 — feature name]
[1–2 sentences explaining the benefit. Specific, not generic.]

2. [Pillar 2]
[1–2 sentences.]

3. [Pillar 3]
[1–2 sentences.]

4. [Pillar 4]
[1–2 sentences.]

In short: [Program Name] isn't about [surface-level promise]. It's a complete [system / methodology] built on [core principle].

On your call today, we'll dive into your specific situation and figure out if it's the right fit.

Talk soon,
[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: By the time they're on the call, they already understand *what* the program is. The call becomes a conversation about *whether it fits them*, not a pitch. Closes go up because the prospect arrives pre-qualified mentally.

---

## Email 4 — Urgency / Scarcity

**Send:** Day of the call (~2 hours before)
**Goal:** Anchor scarcity before the call so the close on the call is clean.

```
Subject: [X] spots left this month

Hey [First Name],

I'm only accepting [X] more people into [Program Name] this month.

Not because I'm trying to be exclusive — but because we limit how many we onboard so every member gets real support.

You've already locked in your call. That tells me something important:
- You're done with [bad behavior].
- You're tired of [pain].
- You're ready to stop [problem] and start [solution].

Here's what makes this program different:
→ [Benefit 1]
→ [Benefit 2]
→ [Benefit 3]

I'm handing you the system and laying out the path.
The only thing I can't give you is the discipline to follow it.

After this call, you can be [end-state transformation].

— [Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: Real scarcity (capacity-based, not artificial) lets you close on the call without resorting to high-pressure tactics. The "I can't give you the discipline" line is a classic — it gives the prospect agency, which paradoxically makes them more likely to commit.

---

## Customization checklist

- [ ] All 4 emails align with the same Program Name and price tier.
- [ ] The "95%" framing in Email 1 matches the actual industry data — don't fabricate stats.
- [ ] The contrarian insight in Email 2 is **actually** contrarian — not a recycled platitude.
- [ ] The 4 pillars in Email 3 match the actual program structure (otherwise the call gets confusing).
- [ ] Email 4's scarcity (X spots) matches reality. If you say 3 spots, you'd better mean 3.
- [ ] All 4 emails reply-able — high-ticket prospects often reply with questions before the call.
- [ ] If the call is rescheduled, the ESP resets timing — test this carefully.

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                               | Fix                                                            |
|-------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Email 1 written as a generic confirmation             | The framing IS the email. Without the 95/5 split, it's just a calendar reminder. |
| Email 2 with no real contrarian point                 | If you can't name what gurus won't say, you're not ready to sell high-ticket. Build authority elsewhere first. |
| Email 3 misaligned with the actual program structure  | The call will expose the misalignment. Refund risk goes up. Match exactly. |
| Email 4's scarcity invented                           | One whiff of fake scarcity tanks the close on the call. Use real capacity numbers only. |
| Sending Email 4 to leads who already paid a deposit   | Tag them. Sending after deposit feels manipulative.            |
