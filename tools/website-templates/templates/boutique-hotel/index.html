Journeys

The Journeys

Exclusive experiences curated for Aurion guests, from cloud forests to volcanic coastlines across Costa Rica's most breathtaking landscapes.

Cloud Forest Expedition

A private guided trek through Monteverde's misty canopy, hanging bridges, rare wildlife encounters, and a chef-prepared dinner in a hidden clearing.

Volcanic Hot Springs

Exclusive access to secluded thermal pools fed by Arenal Volcano, paired with a volcanic mud ritual and open-air massage.

Pacific Coast Sailing

A full-day private charter along the Pacific coastline with snorkelling stops and sunset service on deck.

Rainforest Immersion

Dawn walks with a resident naturalist through primary rainforest, followed by a canopy breakfast.

The Coffee Trail

A highland estate tour from cherry to cup, with a private cupping led by a third-generation grower.

River Safari

A slow drift through mangrove channels at golden hour, spotting caiman, herons, and howler monkeys.

Marine Sanctuary

A guided dive in a protected reserve, surfacing to a floating lunch above the reef.
