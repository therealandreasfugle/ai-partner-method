# 09. Win back

**Send:** about two weeks after they cancel, once the decision is not fresh.
**Goal:** leave the door open, and find out what actually went wrong.

---

**Subject:** No hard feelings, one question

Hi [First name],

Just wanted to say thanks for the last [length of time], no hard feelings at all.

One question, and be honest, it genuinely helps me: was it the money, was it that
you weren't seeing enough out of it, or was it something I did?

Whatever it is I'd rather know.

Your site's yours and it stays yours. If you ever want a hand with it, or you
want to pick the monthly back up, just say. No awkwardness either way.

[Your name]

---

## Three months later, if it went quietly

**Subject:** Still going alright?

Hi [First name],

Been a while. Just wondering how the site's holding up and whether you're still
getting enquiries through it.

If it needs a refresh or you fancy having someone looking after it again, give me
a shout. If not, all good, glad it's still doing a job for you.

[Your name]

---

## Notes

- The question in the first email is the point of sending it. Three or four
  honest answers will change your business more than any course will.
- Never argue with the reason they give.
- ⚠️ Never hold their site, their domain or their content hostage on the way out.
  Hand over everything cleanly. A client who leaves happy refers people. A client
  who leaves angry tells the whole trade.
- The three month email is what actually wins people back. The first one is for
  information.
