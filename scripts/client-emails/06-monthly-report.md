# 06. Monthly report

**Send:** the same date every month, forever, whether or not they ask.
**Goal:** they remember what they pay for, without opening a dashboard.

This is the most important email in this folder. A client who hears from you
monthly stays for years. A client who hears nothing cancels the first quiet month
they have.

---

**Subject:** [Month] numbers for [Business name]

Hi [First name],

Here's how [month] went.

**[number] people visited your site**, [up/down] from [number] last month.

**[number] of those got in touch.** That's the number that turns into work, so
it's the one I watch.

**Most of them came from [Google / your Google listing / social].**

**The pages they read most were [page] and [page].**

What I did this month:
- [one thing]
- [one thing]

What I'm doing next month:
- [one thing]

Anything you want changed or added, just reply.

[Your name]

---

## Where the numbers come from

All four are in their GA4 property. See `04-deliver/10-analytics`. Do not send
them a link to GA4 itself, they will open it once and never again.

Enquiry count is the one that matters. If you can, cross-check it against the
lead emails that actually went to them, because that is the number they will
compare against their own memory of the month.

## Notes

- **Never skip a quiet month.** A month with bad numbers still gets this email,
  with an honest line about why and what you are changing. Silence reads as
  neglect, bad numbers read as honesty.
- The "what I did" section is the part that keeps you paid. The first four
  numbers prove the site works. This part proves you work.
- In a genuinely quiet month you still have something: a speed check, a new
  service page, updated seasonal copy, fresh photos, a schema fix.
- ⚠️ Report what happened. Never promise what next month will do.
