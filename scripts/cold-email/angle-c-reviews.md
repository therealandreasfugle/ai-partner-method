# Angle C: reviews

Use when they have very few reviews, or a good rating they are clearly not
making anything of.

⚠️ **Only use a number if you actually looked.** "You have 11 reviews" when they
have 40 ends the conversation and deserves to. If you have not checked, write the
version without a number.

---

> a reputation problem. Nobody gets defensive about a process gap.
