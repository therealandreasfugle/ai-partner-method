# 10. Stripe

**What it does for you:** takes the money. Both the one time build fee and the
monthly retainer.

**Cost:** no monthly fee. They take a small cut of each payment.

## Steps

1. Sign up at https://dashboard.stripe.com/register
2. Complete the business details. You can start taking payments before this is
   fully verified, but you cannot get paid out until it is done, so do it now.
3. Create two payment links at https://dashboard.stripe.com/payment-links

   - **The build fee.** One time. Whatever you have agreed.
   - **The monthly plan.** Recurring, monthly. Make one link per tier you sell.

4. Paste the links into your proposal's seller settings panel.

## How it fits the sale

The proposal page has a contract the client signs on the page. Payment is
separate, and it happens in the same conversation:

1. They sign the agreement on the proposal
2. You send the payment link, or open it on screen with them
3. Payment clears
4. You send the onboarding form the same day

⚠️ **Do not start work before the money clears.** The agreement says the build fee
is non-refundable once work commences, which only protects you if the sequence is
in that order.

⚠️ Never promise a refund. The guarantee in the proposal is that you keep working
until the client is happy, not that they get their money back. Those are very
different promises and only one of them is safe.
