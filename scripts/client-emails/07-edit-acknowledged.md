# 07. Edit acknowledged

**Send:** within the hour. Every time. Even if you cannot do the work for two
days.
**Goal:** they never wonder whether you saw it.

---

**Subject:** Reply to their email, do not start a new one

Got it, I'll have that changed by [day].

[Your name]

---

That is the whole email. Do not add anything to it.

---

## If it is bigger than an edit

**Subject:** Reply to theirs

Got it. That one's a bit more than a quick change, so it'll take me until [day].

Anything else you want doing while I'm in there, send it over now and I'll do it
all in one go.

[Your name]

---

## Notes

- Two lines, sent in twenty seconds, is the single cheapest loyalty you will ever
  buy. A same-day reply to "can you change the phone number" is worth more than
  any report.
- Give a day, not "soon". Then beat it.
- Priority edits are included up to the hour on their plan. Past that it is
  charged hourly and it is in the agreement, so raise it before you do the work,
  not on the invoice.
- ⚠️ Never let an edit request sit overnight unanswered. That is how a happy
  client starts wondering what they pay you for.
