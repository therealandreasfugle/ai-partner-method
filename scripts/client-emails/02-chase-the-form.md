# 02. Chase the form

**Send:** two days after email 01, if the form has not come back.
**Goal:** get the answers without nagging. Most stalled builds are a form nobody
filled in.

---

**Subject:** Want me to fill this in with you?

Hi [First name],

No rush at all, but I know a form is the last thing you want after a day on the
tools.

If it's easier, give me ten minutes on the phone and I'll fill it in while we
talk. Most of it I can answer just from looking at your Google listing anyway,
I really only need the bits I can't guess.

[booking link]

Or if you'd rather just crack on with it yourself, it's here:

[onboarding form link]

Either's fine.

[Your name]

---

## Notes

- Never send a second chase with a guilt line in it. If this one does not land,
  ring them.
- The offer to do it together is the whole email. That is what converts, not the
  reminder.
- If they go quiet past a week, call. Do not email a third time.
