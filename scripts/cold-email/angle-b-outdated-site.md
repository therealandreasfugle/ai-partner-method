# Angle B: their website is old or broken on a phone

Harder than angle A, because they already spent money once and may be defensive
about it. Somebody built that site. Sometimes it was their nephew. Never insult
it directly.

The move is to talk about **what it costs them now**, not what is wrong with it.

---

## Email 4: the phone test

**Subject:** {{business}} on a phone

Hi,

Most people who look up {{business}} are doing it on a phone, usually standing
in the room with the problem.

I rebuilt yours so it works properly on one: {{site}}

Same information, just easier to act on. Have a look on your phone and tell me
what you think.

{{your_name}}

> **Why this works.** "Standing in the room with the problem" is the whole pitch
> in eight words. It puts the owner in the customer's shoes without a lecture.
> ⚠️ Only send this if you actually opened their site on a phone width. If it is
> already fine on mobile, use angle D instead. A false observation kills the
> whole thread.

Hi,

not doing better work than you. They are just easier to trust in the five
seconds somebody spends deciding.

Here is what {{business}} looks like built the same way: {{site}}

Want me to walk you through the difference?

{{your_name}}

> **Why this works.** "Not doing better work than you" is the most reliable line
> in local business outreach. It is almost always true, they already believe it,
> and it makes you the only person who has said it out loud.

## Email 6: the one thing

**Subject:** One thing on your site

Hi,

Your site tells people what you do. What it does not do is make it easy for them
to get hold of you in the moment they decide, which is the only moment that
matters.

I rebuilt it with that fixed: {{site}}

Five minutes on the phone and I can show you what changed and why.

{{your_name}}

> **Why this works.** It is specific and fixable, and it is true of nearly every
> old local business site. It also sets up the enquiry form and the instant alert
> as the thing they are buying, which is what they actually are buying.
