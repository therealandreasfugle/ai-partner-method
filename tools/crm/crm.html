How this works

  1. Your leads live in your Google Sheet. Every scrape adds more, and everything you do here saves back to it.
  2. Power dial is built for your desk. Sit at your computer and work the list one lead at a time: the script is on screen, click Call, the call rings out on your own number through your phone, then click what happened. Filter first and it dials just that list.
  3. Emails send themselves once a day to leads with an email address. The Email health tab shows how they are landing.
  4. Replies float to the top. Answer them fast; that is where the deals are.
  5. Board view is your pipeline. Drag a lead between columns as it progresses; on a phone, open the lead and change its status instead.
Call from this computer (one-time setup)
  • Mac + iPhone: open the Mac's Phone app (older Macs: FaceTime settings) and finish its setup, and on the iPhone allow Settings, Phone, Calls on Other Devices.
  • Windows + Android: install Microsoft's free Phone Link app and pair your phone once.
  • Windows + iPhone: Phone Link pairs over Bluetooth for basic calling.

Calls go out on your own number and cost nothing extra. No computer around? Open this page on your phone and tap Call instead.
