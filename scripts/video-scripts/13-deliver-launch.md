# Video 13: Deliver line 08, launch, domain, hosting and SSL

**Length:** 4 minutes. **Value on the proposal:** $500.
**Source:** `04-deliver/08-launch`.

---

## OPEN

**SAY THIS:**

> Their site actually live on their own web address, secure, with the first ninety
> days covered. There is nothing to build here. It is four steps and a wait.

---

## SCREEN

1. Deploy their site to its own Vercel project.
2. In that project, Settings, then Domains. Add their domain.
3. Vercel shows you the DNS records. Add those wherever the domain is registered.
4. Wait. The padlock turns itself on.

TALK ABOUT:
- If they do not have a domain, buy one for them and bill it on. About ten a year.
- A project of their own, every time. Never share a Vercel project between two
  clients.

---

## YOU MUST DO

- Confirm the live domain is serving the new site, not a holding page
- Check the padlock is there before you tell anybody it is live

**SAY THIS:**

> A successful deploy message is not proof of anything. Open the actual address in
> a browser and look at it. Twice I have seen somebody tell a client it was live
> while the domain was still pointed at the old holding page.

---

## THE ONE THING

This is the one that turns a routine exit into a bad review. Say it slowly.

**SAY THIS:**

> Never move a client's domain into your own account without telling them. If they
> ever leave, that domain is theirs and they will need it. Either get added as a
> user on their registrar account, or stay on the phone with them while they add
> the DNS records themselves.
>
> Quietly taking control of somebody's domain is the single fastest way to turn a
> normal parting of ways into a public argument, and they will be right.
