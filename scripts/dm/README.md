# DM scripts

## Why this channel exists

The scraper deliberately keeps leads that have a Facebook or Instagram page but
no email address. On a real 1,700 lead sweep of the West Midlands, 748 leads had
a social profile. If you only ever send email, you are ignoring a large slice of
your own list, and it is the slice with the least competition in the inbox.

## The rules

1. **A DM is a conversation, not an email.** Never paste an email into a DM. It
   reads as a bot instantly and gets you reported.
2. **Two lines, then stop.** Send a short message, wait for a reply, then send
   the link. Leading with a link is the single fastest way to get filtered.
3. **Message the business page, not the owner's personal account**, unless the
   business page is obviously the personal account.
4. **Never send more than 20 to 30 a day from a new account.** Instagram and
   Facebook both throttle and then restrict. This is a slow channel, not a
   volume channel.
5. **Comment or react on a recent post before you message.** It puts you in their
   notifications as a person first. Takes five seconds and materially changes
   the reply rate.
6. No emoji, no dashes, no capital letter openers like "Hey there!!". Write the
   way you would text somebody you half know.

## The two step move

Every script below is in two parts, because **the first message never contains a
link**. You are buying a reply, not a click.

---

## Script 1: no website (the default)

**Message one**

> hi, do you handle the enquiries for {{business}} or is there someone else i
> should speak to?

**When they reply**

> nice one. i build websites for {{trade}} businesses around {{town}} and i put
> one together for you already so you can just look at it rather than me
> describing it
>
> want me to send it over?

**When they say yes**

> here you go: {{site}}
>
> nothing signed up for, not public anywhere. have a look and tell me straight
> if it is not for you

> **Why the first message works.** "Do you handle the enquiries" is a real
> question with a one word answer, and it is flattering rather than salesy. It
> gets a reply from people who would never reply to a pitch.

---

## Script 2: they post their work

Use when their page is full of finished job photos, which is common for trades,
detailers, landscapers and salons.

**Message one**

> the (thing from their most recent post) came out really well. do you get much
> work off here or is it mostly word of mouth?

**When they reply**

> makes sense. the reason i ask is your work is better than the way people find
> it. i build sites for {{trade}} businesses and i made you one already
>
> want to see it?

> ⚠️ You have to actually look at the post. Reference the real job. A generic
> "love your work" opener gets ignored and deserves to.
>
> "Your work is better than the way people find it" is the line that converts.
> It is a compliment and a problem in the same sentence.

---

## Script 3: they are clearly busy and successful

Do not lead with a problem. They do not have one.

**Message one**

> hi, quick one. are you taking on new work at the moment or are you booked up?

**If booked up**

> figured. most of the good ones are
>
> when it does go quiet, the businesses that fill back up fastest are the ones
> people can find without knowing the name already. that is the bit i build
>
> i made you one to look at if you ever want it: {{site}}

> **Why this works.** You agree with them, then plant the one fear a booked
> tradesperson actually has, which is the quiet month that always comes. No
> pressure, no ask. A surprising number come back weeks later.

---

## Script 4: the follow up

Send once, three or four days later. If nothing, stop. Do not DM a third time.

> no worries if not interested, just checking you saw this. happy to leave it
> with you either way

---

## What to do when they reply "how much"

Do not answer with a number in a DM. A price in a chat window with no context
sounds expensive whatever it is.

> depends what you need, and i would be guessing if i said a number before
> seeing how you get your work at the moment
>
> quickest thing is a ten minute call. what number is best?

Then get them on the phone and follow `scripts/phone/discovery-call.md`.
