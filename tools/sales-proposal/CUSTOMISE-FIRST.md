# Customise this before you send it to anyone

This proposal is a working example, filled in with real copy so you can see what a
finished one looks like. It is signed by Dan, because it is his. **It is not yours
until you change the seven things below.**

Sending it as-is means signing your proposal with someone else's name. Clients notice.

Open `proposal.html` and search for each item:

| Search for | Change it to |
|---|---|
| `I'm Dan.` | Your first name |
| `agency-personal-sig-name">Dan` | Your first name (the signature) |
| `Dan &middot; Founder of` | Your first name (the photo caption) |
| `alt="Dan, the agency Founder"` | Your name (the image alt text) |
| `Dan checked` and `Dan built the new site` | Your name, in the two reviews |
| `{{YOUR_BUSINESS}}` | Your business name, everywhere it appears |
| `agency-assets/your-photo.jpg` | Drop in a photo of yourself at that path |

## The reviews

The four reviews are real ones written about Dan's work. **Replace them with your own
as soon as you have any.** Until then, either delete the review block entirely or say
plainly on the call that they are your mentor's results, not yours.

Do not present someone else's reviews as your own. It is the fastest way to lose a
client who checks, and they do check.

## Check before you send

Search the file for `Dan` one last time. If you get any hits outside this document,
you are not finished.
