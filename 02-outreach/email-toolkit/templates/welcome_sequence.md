# Welcome Sequence (3 emails)

**Goal:** Introduce the brand, build belief, drive the first action (book a call, buy entry-level offer, or click the main CTA).

**Trigger:** Subscriber opts in (newsletter, lead magnet, free training, etc.).

**Recommended framework:** AIDA for #1 (cold-ish — they just opted in but don't know you yet), BAB for #2, direct ask for #3.

---

## Email 1 — Hey / Introduction

**Send:** Immediately after opt-in
**Framework:** AIDA
**Goal:** Set expectation, plant the offer.

```
Subject: Hey

Hey [First Name],

How are you feeling? I hope you're motivated and ready to make moves.

You went down this [industry / topic] rabbit hole, and whether you've been [doing X] for years or just getting started, something tells you this path is for you.

You've seen [result or outcome the audience cares about] change lives — including mine — and you're ready for it to change yours too.

But maybe there's a little skepticism holding you back. I get it. If it were easy, everyone would do it.

When I started, I had no idea I'd go from [starting point] to [credibility result]. My students went from [pain] to [outcome] — all by mastering [skill / system].

Imagine how quickly you could see real progress if you had someone in your corner telling you what to do every step of the way.

This [offer / resource / next step] will:
- [Outcome 1 — specific]
- [Outcome 2]
- [Outcome 3 — the deepest one, the transformation]
- [Outcome 4 — the credibility-by-association one]

You can [see it / get it / book the call] here: [Link]

P.S. I'll be sending you regular [content type] over the next few days. If you're reading this now, you're still early.

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: "Hey" as a subject feels personal, drives high opens. The body builds belief through specificity (your story, your students' results) before any ask. The P.S. sets the cadence — reduces unsubscribes from people surprised by future emails.

---

## Email 2 — Success Story

**Send:** Day 2
**Framework:** BAB
**Goal:** Prove the system works through someone they can identify with.

```
Subject: How [Client Name] made [specific result] after [timeframe]

Hey [First Name],

I'm tired of [target audience] coming to me who fell for the [bad alternative they tried first].

They don't work.

What you should be looking for is someone who has [credibility marker — verifiable].

I've helped [audience] achieve [result]. And here's the thing —
[Industry / niche] is a game of details, and most people skip them.

[Brief personal story — your struggle, your turning point. 3–4 sentences.]

Like [Client Name 1] who [specific result with numbers].
Or [Client Name 2] who [specific result].

All of this is possible. I'm not saying it's guaranteed — you have to put in the work.

I'm currently taking [X] people to work with me [1-on-1 / in the cohort / etc.].

If you want to see if you're a fit:
Apply here: [Link]

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: Calls out the bad alternatives the reader already tried — proves you understand their world. The two named client results are specific (numbers, timeframes). The "I'm not saying it's guaranteed" line builds trust by NOT overclaiming.

>>> Don't have client results to name? Don't fabricate. Use the BAB framework with your *own* before/after instead, and add the case study email later when you have real student wins.

---

## Email 3 — Direct Ask

**Send:** Day 3
**Framework:** Direct
**Goal:** Get them to book / buy / click. Closes the welcome window.

```
Subject: Re: your call

Hey [First Name],

I'm looking for [X] people who want to [specific outcome] without [common obstacle].

I recently opened enrollments for [program / offer] again after it was closed.

For serious [audience]: I'm going to personally [mentor / work with / guide] you to build your [system / outcome] in a way that you can see the path to [end state].

You've seen my results, [First Name].
You've seen how I've helped [audience] [achieve specific result].
You've seen [proof point — case study, testimonial, public result].

If you're ready for the next step:

Set up a time here: [Link]

See you there.

[Sender Name]
```

>>> Why this works: Day 3 is when newly-warmed leads convert highest. The "Re:" subject implies a continuation (you've been talking with them — and you have, via Email 1 and 2). The list of "you've seen" lines compounds the credibility built earlier in the sequence.

---

## Customization checklist

- [ ] Email 1's "list of outcomes" is from your brief — pulled directly from the offer description.
- [ ] Email 2's case studies use **real** client names and **real** numbers.
- [ ] Email 3's CTA goes to the same destination as Email 1's CTA (consistency builds belief).
- [ ] Personal token `{{first_name}}` placed in subject and first line of body.
- [ ] If the brief specifies `audience_temperature: hot`, drop Email 1's AIDA structure — they don't need the long hook. Use a shorter PAS opener instead.
- [ ] Send all three within 72 hours of opt-in. Past that, they cool off.

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                            | Fix                                                       |
|----------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Sending all 3 emails on day 1                      | Stagger by 24h. The cadence is the experience.            |
| Email 2 with vague results ("amazing transformations") | Use specific numbers and named clients only.            |
| Email 3 too aggressive ("LAST CHANCE!!!")          | Direct ≠ desperate. Keep tone calm and confident.         |
| Using "Re:" subject when there's no actual prior thread | Works in this sequence (you HAVE been talking — via Email 1+2). Don't use it cold. |
| No P.S. on Email 1                                 | The P.S. is the second-most-read element. Use it for cadence-setting or a soft CTA. |
